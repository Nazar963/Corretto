Corretto

A free browser extension for Chrome, Edge, Brave and Firefox.

Corretto adds a small button to text fields. Click it to get three rewrites of your text (a minimal fix, a more fluid version and a more formal one) in the same language, then pick one to replace your text. You can undo it at any time.

It works with an AI provider you choose and your own API key, including free tiers and models running on your own computer. Corretto has no server and collects no data.
